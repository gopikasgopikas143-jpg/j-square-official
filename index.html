Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find your answer? Call our expert team directly.

How long does legal verification take?+
Typically 3–5 working days for a complete check. Urgent cases can be fast-tracked within 48 hours.
Is your valuation report accepted by banks?+
Yes. Our reports are accepted by SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis Bank, and most cooperative banks.
Do you offer services outside Salem?+
We primarily serve Salem, Erode, and Namakkal districts. Call us to assess other locations.
Can I get Vastu consultation for an existing home?+
Absolutely. We offer both pre-purchase and post-construction Vastu consultations with remediation advice.
How is your consultation fee structured?+
First call is always free. All fees are transparent and disclosed upfront — no hidden charges ever.
